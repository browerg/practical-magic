# How to edit your website

Hi Kayla — you can now change your own rates, post an away-notice, and add
testimonials without waiting on me. Here's everything you need.

---

## Getting in

1. Go to **[your-site]/admin**
2. Click **Login with GitHub**
3. That's it — you'll see the editor.

*(One-time setup: you'll need a free GitHub account, and I'll send you an invite
to accept. After that, it's just the login button.)*

Bookmark the /admin page so you don't have to remember it.

---

## Making a change

Everything lives under **Site Content → Rates, Notice & Testimonials**.

Click into it, make your changes, then hit **Publish → Publish now** at the top.
Your website updates itself in about a minute. Refresh the site to see it live.

---

## What you can change

### The notice banner
That "Please note" box on the front page.

- **Show the banner** — flip this ON when you're away or have an announcement,
  OFF when you're back. When it's off, the box disappears from the site entirely.
- **Banner text** — whatever you want it to say.
  Example: *Services unavailable Aug 20–Sept 3. Reach out for alternative resources.*

Don't type "Please note:" — that part is already built in.

### Rate cards
Three groups: Pet Care, Home Cleaning, Yard Maintenance.

- Change a price by typing over it. Write it however you want it to appear:
  `$25`, `$60/night`, `$150–$320` all work.
- **Add a service**: click **Add** at the bottom of the list.
- **Remove one**: click the **✕** on that row.
- **Reorder**: drag the rows.
- **Fine print** is the small italic text under each card.

### Testimonials
Kind words from clients.

- Click **Add** to create one. Fill in the quote and the name
  (e.g. *Sarah & Biscuit*).
- Don't add quotation marks — they're added automatically.
- **This section stays hidden on your site until you add at least one.** As soon
  as you publish the first, a "Kind Words" section appears between the cleaning
  photos and the booking form.

---

## Good to know

- **Nothing breaks.** If a change looks wrong, tell me — every edit is saved in
  history and I can undo any of it.
- **Changes aren't instant.** Give it a minute after publishing, then refresh.
- **Photos and wording** elsewhere on the site (the About text, service
  descriptions, galleries) aren't in the editor yet — send those to me and I'll
  take care of it.
- **Bookings still come to your email** exactly as before. Nothing about the
  contact form changed.
